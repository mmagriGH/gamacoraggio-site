PRIVACY · PRE-RELEASE PREVIEW

EnduroStrength Privacy

Last updated: October 22, 2026. EnduroStrength is in development and is not available for general download. This is a provisional privacy notice describing anticipated product behavior, not a statement that integrations or data collection are active. It will be updated to match the actual release before App Store submission.

Planned local training information

EnduroStrength is designed to keep training programs, completed strength sessions, exercise preferences, and progress locally on the device by default. Exact storage, export, and deletion controls will be documented before release.

Optional connected athlete data

Apple Health access may be offered for relevant training context. If included, EnduroStrength will request permission through iOS and explain the Health data types and purpose. We do not intend to use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage.

Planned Garmin Connect connection — not active

GamaCoraggio has requested Garmin Connect developer access. If integration is approved and implemented, connecting Garmin will be optional and require the user's authorization. Based on feature and permission scope, data may include completed activities, training and recovery context, supported wellness metrics, and compatible planned workouts submitted at the user's request.

Garmin integration may require secure server processing of authentication credentials, delivery messages, and limited data. The hosting provider, data categories, exact retention periods, security and deletion mechanisms have not yet been finalized. We will disclose the actual providers and processes before activating Garmin connectivity.

We do not plan to send Garmin-derived health or workout data to advertising platforms, TelemetryDeck product-usage analytics, or unrelated AI services. Users will have an option to disconnect Garmin. A future product-specific notice will explain whether disconnection removes previously imported information and how users may request deletion.

Planned product analytics

We plan to use a privacy-conscious service such as TelemetryDeck to understand broad feature usage and improve the application, potentially including anonymous installation identifiers and general product-interaction events. The final categories, processor, consent flow and opt-out controls will be disclosed before data collection begins. Detailed exercise performance values and Garmin or HealthKit records are not intended as analytics event properties.

Purchases, retention and deletion

Optional premium subscriptions may be offered through Apple's App Store. GamaCoraggio would receive entitlement information, not payment-card details. Any final local and server retention, data export and deletion controls will be described before launch.

Privacy questions
