ENDUROSTRENGTH · PRIVACY POLICY

Your training belongs to you.

Last updated: November 12, 2026. GamaCoraggio LLC develops EnduroStrength for iPhone. This policy describes the data practices implemented for EnduroStrength 1.0 and its related website. Any materially different features introduced in future versions will require updated disclosures.

Information you choose to save

EnduroStrength stores your athlete profile, preferred sport, available equipment, weekly training targets, manually entered schedule and race context, training limitations, workout recommendations, workout records and history, exercise preferences, customized circuits, routines and progress on your iPhone. Workout data may include repetitions, external loads, completed sets and session feedback. An EnduroStrength account is not required.

This training data is stored locally by default. EnduroStrength 1.0 does not operate a GamaCoraggio cloud account or continuously synchronize training history between devices. Apple device backup services may include eligible local app data depending on your settings and Apple's backup policies.

Optional, consent-based product analytics

Usage analytics are off by default. You can explicitly choose to enable or disable them during onboarding or in More → Data & Privacy. If enabled, EnduroStrength uses TelemetryDeck to send limited app interaction events, an anonymized installation identifier and general app or device information to understand feature usage and improve the product.

We do not intentionally include your name, personal workout notes, health or limitation details, symptoms, exact exercise loads or repetitions in analytics events. Turning analytics off stops future analytics events from the app, but does not recall data already transmitted. TelemetryDeck processes analytics information according to its own applicable privacy and retention practices.

Purchases and Pro subscriptions

Apple processes all in-app purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions through StoreKit. EnduroStrength uses verified Apple transaction and entitlement information to determine whether Pro features are available. GamaCoraggio does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Subscription management, cancellations, billing and refunds are handled through your Apple Account and Apple's services.

Data export, recovery and deletion

More → Data & Privacy lets you export a training archive containing your profile, program, schedule, training limitations, routines and workout records. You can also separately export completed workout history. Export is available without Pro.

The exported training archive is a readable JSON file and is not encrypted by EnduroStrength. Protect the file, avoid sharing it publicly and store it somewhere you trust. A saved archive can be restored on the first welcome screen of a fresh installation; EnduroStrength 1.0 cannot merge an archive into an existing profile. Do not delete the app merely to test restoration.

You may change or delete local preferences and workout records through available app controls. Removing the app removes its locally stored data from that device, subject to any Apple-managed device backups or files you previously exported. To request deletion of information you voluntarily sent to us by email, contact us using the address below.

Apple Health, Garmin and other external integrations

EnduroStrength 1.0 does not connect to Apple Health, Garmin Connect, Strava or TrainingPeaks, and does not provide iCloud workout synchronization. These should not be assumed to be active services. If optional integrations launch later, we will explain the permissions, information types, providers and controls before enabling them.

Website visits and support requests

Our public pages are hosted through third-party infrastructure, including Cloudflare. Standard delivery and security logs may include IP address, browser details and request information. If you contact support, we receive the email address, message and any information you choose to provide, solely to answer your request and manage related support. Please do not send medical records or other sensitive information by ordinary email.

Children's privacy and fitness guidance

EnduroStrength is a general fitness product and is not designed as a service directed to children. We do not knowingly request children's personal information through an app account. Training recommendations are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app functionality or practices change. The revised date will appear here. Privacy inquiries: legal@gamacoraggio.com. General help: EnduroStrength Support.
