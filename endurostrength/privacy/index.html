ENDUROSTRENGTH · VERSION 1.0

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026. This notice describes the intended EnduroStrength 1.0 privacy behavior and must be reviewed before release. EnduroStrength is provided by GamaCoraggio LLC.

Local-first training data

EnduroStrength stores your name or nickname (if provided), sport and equipment choices, workout preferences, date-specific endurance schedule, training adjustments, program and completed workout history locally on your iPhone. Version 1.0 does not require an EnduroStrength account or provide EnduroStrength-operated cloud synchronization. The app's core exercise and training guidance does not require continuous internet access.

Your iPhone operating system and backup settings may preserve app data in device backups. Removing the app ordinarily removes its local data from the device but does not necessarily erase backup copies that Apple or the user may retain.

Workout history export and limitations

You can use More → Data & Privacy → Export Workout History to save your completed workouts as a JSON document through the iOS file exporter, without purchasing Pro. This feature exports completed workout records only. It does not export every preference, training context entry or in-progress session, and it is not an EnduroStrength cloud backup.

Optional product usage analytics

Usage analytics are off by default. If you opt in, EnduroStrength uses TelemetryDeck to process limited product-interaction events and basic app/device metadata to understand reliability and feature adoption. Data can include an app installation identifier, approximate event timing, feature names, subscription-related events and the general success or failure of workflows.

EnduroStrength filters event properties. We do not intentionally send your name, workout notes, symptoms, body-region limitations, exact repetitions or weights as TelemetryDeck event properties. We do not use training data for targeted advertisements or sell workout history to advertisers.

You can disable future app-initiated usage analytics in More → Data & Privacy. Turning off analytics cannot retract previously transmitted records. TelemetryDeck's infrastructure and applicable data processing practices are separate from the data stored locally by EnduroStrength.

App Store subscriptions

Apple handles Pro subscription payment, pricing, trial eligibility, renewal, cancellation and purchase restoration through StoreKit and the App Store. EnduroStrength receives and checks signed product transaction/entitlement information to unlock Pro. GamaCoraggio does not receive your full payment-card details from the app.

Apple Health and Garmin

EnduroStrength 1.0 does not offer active Apple Health or Garmin Connect synchronization. Future optional integrations, if introduced, will require an updated privacy notice and appropriate permission and controls before activation.

Support and questions

For privacy questions, data concerns or requests, email legal@gamacoraggio.com. For general support email support@gamacoraggio.com. Do not include sensitive medical records in routine support emails.

Also see GamaCoraggio's corporate privacy notice and EnduroStrength Terms of Use.
