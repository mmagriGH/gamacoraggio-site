Who is EnduroStrength for?

EnduroStrength offers general strength training for anyone looking to get stronger, with optional training context designed for running, cycling, swimming and triathlon. Choose your preferred sport, equipment and training goals during onboarding.

Where do I start a workout?

Open Today for your recommended session, then choose Start Workout. Use Board to view and log the full workout or Guided for one exercise at a time with coaching cues and photos. You can change set values before logging.

What is included in Free and Pro?

Free supports strength workouts, workout history, training adjustments, starter sport drills and export. Pro adds advanced workout customization, more planning options, guided mobility and recovery sessions, expanded sport drills and additional insights. Current prices and any free-trial eligibility are shown in the Apple purchase screen.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

On your iPhone, open EnduroStrength and go to the Pro or subscription section under More, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for your purchase. If your subscription is no longer active, review its status in iPhone Settings or at Apple Subscriptions.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Apple manages billing and renewal. To cancel or change a subscription, use Manage Subscriptions. For eligible App Store refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting EnduroStrength does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

Where are my workouts stored?

Your profile, program, schedules, workout history and saved routines are stored locally on your iPhone. No EnduroStrength account or automatic multi-device cloud sync is required in version 1.0. Keep iPhone Backup enabled where appropriate.

How can I export or restore training data?

Go to More → Data & Privacy and export your training archive or workout history. The training archive is a JSON file not encrypted by the app; protect it. Archive restoration is available only on the initial welcome screen of a fresh installation, and cannot overwrite or merge with an existing athlete profile. Do not uninstall your only copy of the app just to test recovery.

Can I use Apple Health or Garmin Connect?

Apple Health, Garmin Connect, Strava, TrainingPeaks and automatic iCloud training sync are not active integrations in EnduroStrength 1.0.

Does EnduroStrength send analytics?

Optional TelemetryDeck product-usage analytics are off by default. You control consent through onboarding and More → Data & Privacy. The Privacy Policy explains what is sent if you opt in.

Where can I find exercise guidance?

Choose an exercise to view technique photos, equipment and coaching notes. Training guidance is for general fitness only, and does not replace advice from a qualified medical professional. Stop if a movement causes pain or concerning symptoms.

Contact the development team

Email support@gamacoraggio.com with the issue, the iOS version, your EnduroStrength app version and the steps needed to reproduce it. You can find the app version in More → Support. Please avoid sending sensitive medical records, personal health details, or unencrypted training archives by email.
