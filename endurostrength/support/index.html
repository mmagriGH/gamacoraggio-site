ENDUROSTRENGTH · SUPPORT

Help with your training.

Why does my schedule say "Not set"?

EnduroStrength does not automatically import your training calendar in version 1.0. Open Today or Plan and enter your endurance load for the day. Unknown training days are handled conservatively rather than assumed to be easy.

Why did my suggested workout change?

The program takes your selected sport, equipment, endurance training and active training adjustments into account. You can review and customize the next workout, but safety-related constraints may limit additional exercises or volume.

How do I unlock or restore Pro?

Open More → EnduroStrength Pro. Use the App Store purchase sheet to subscribe, or tap Restore Purchases if you have an active subscription associated with your Apple Account. If a completed purchase remains locked, check your subscriptions in Apple Account settings before purchasing again.

How can I export my history?

Open More → Data & Privacy → Export Workout History. You can save completed workouts as JSON at no charge. The export is not a full backup of your entire training configuration.

Is EnduroStrength medical care?

No. It provides general exercise education. Stop activity if painful or unsafe. If you have concerning symptoms, consult a qualified professional; do not use the app as a substitute for medical care.
