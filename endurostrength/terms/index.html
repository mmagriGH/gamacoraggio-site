Terms of Use

Last updated November 7, 2026. These draft Terms describe use of EnduroStrength version 1.0, offered by GamaCoraggio LLC. They are intended for review before the app is published.

Use of the app

EnduroStrength offers general fitness planning, strength, mobility, recovery and sport-skills education. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, medical treatment or physical therapy. The app cannot evaluate your individual health status or guarantee results, performance improvements or injury prevention.

Use only movements and equipment appropriate for your experience and circumstances. Stop exercise when experiencing pain, dizziness or other concerning symptoms and seek appropriate qualified advice. Training suggestions and check-in adjustments cannot replace professional evaluation when needed.

Free access and subscriptions

Free functionality includes daily strength recommendations, training adjustments, starter sports drills, completed workout history and workout-history export. Pro adds weekly planning, mobility/recovery routines, custom workouts and expanded sport-skill guidance, subject to the then-current app features and eligible access.

Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Subscription prices, periods and introductory-trial eligibility are displayed at the time of purchase. If eligible, a free trial converts into the paid subscription unless canceled. Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, subject to Apple's published subscription terms. You can manage and cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting EnduroStrength does not cancel an Apple subscription.

When a trial or subscription expires, Pro access may end, but existing completed workout history remains available in Free, subject to local data retention on the device. Refund decisions and payment support are handled by Apple under its applicable policies.

Data and device limitations

Version 1.0 stores core data locally and does not offer EnduroStrength account-based backup, migration or cloud synchronization. Use the available completed-workout export control to save a limited copy of your completed workout records. An export is not a complete backup of all app data.

Updates and availability

We may adjust supported devices, exercise libraries, feature availability, safety rules, presentation or subscription offerings as the app evolves. Features depend on device software, permissions, App Store connectivity when purchasing and access to supported platform services.

App interface content, designs and exercise guidance are provided for personal use. Do not attempt to distribute an unauthorized commercial copy of the app, or deliberately interfere with its operation. Your rights under applicable law and Apple's terms remain unaffected.
