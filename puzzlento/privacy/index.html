PUZZLENTO · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, at your pace.

Last updated: October 26, 2026. Puzzlento is an offline-first iOS logic puzzle app developed by GamaCoraggio LLC. This policy reflects the pre-release v1.0 design. We will revise it if features or data practices change before launch.

What stays on your device

No account or login is needed. Your puzzle boards, completion history, unfinished games, preferred appearance and sound settings, and daily-puzzle history are saved locally for offline use. The app does not upload your puzzle answers or play history to GamaCoraggio servers. Apple-managed backups may include local app data depending on your settings.

Analytics and tracking

In v1.0, Puzzlento does not send optional analytics events to us or a third-party analytics service. The optional usage-insights setting records only a local preference and does not transmit events. Puzzlento contains no advertising SDK, ad tracking or third-party social login. If analytics are added later, the app's controls and this policy will be updated before collection begins.

Purchases

An optional one-time lifetime collection unlock is processed by Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive credit-card details through the app. Apple handles payment and associated purchase information under Apple's own privacy terms. Puzzlento v1.0 does not offer subscriptions.

Sharing, support and website visits

Sharing is initiated by you via the iOS share sheet. Opening external support/privacy links uses your browser. Web hosting providers may process normal access logs under their own policies. If you voluntarily email our contact address, we receive your address and message to respond and address your request. Please do not include sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Local game data persists until removed. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device, subject to any separately retained Apple device backups. You may change appearance, sound and suggestions at any time. You can disable optional usage insights; the v1.0 app currently transmits no such events. You can contact us to request deletion of information you previously provided by email.

Permissions and children's privacy

Puzzlento does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, Apple Health or Garmin Connect access. No player profile is necessary. Please do not send personal information about children through support emails.

Questions

For privacy requests, email legal@gamacoraggio.com. Visit Puzzlento Support for gameplay and purchase help. See the GamaCoraggio privacy notice.
