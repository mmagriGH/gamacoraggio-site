Puzzlento Privacy

Last updated: October 22, 2026. Puzzlento is in development. This describes the planned privacy approach and is not a claim that the app or its services have launched. A definitive policy will be published before App Store release.

Offline-first puzzle play

Puzzlento is designed for offline play without requiring an account. Puzzle progress, preferences and completed games are intended to remain locally on the device by default. Exact storage and deletion controls will be disclosed when the application is ready to launch.

Planned analytics

GamaCoraggio plans to include privacy-conscious product analytics to learn which puzzle types and features people use, understand reliability and improve the experience. This may involve a service such as TelemetryDeck, which processes anonymous installation identifiers, event timestamps, app and device metadata and broad feature-interaction events. We do not intend to send puzzle contents, individual answers, personal notes or other detailed play records to analytics platforms.

Any final analytics configuration, consent and opt-out controls, service providers, retention practices and the app's actual App Store data-collection disclosure will be published before release.

No Garmin or Apple Health integration planned

Puzzlento is not planned to connect to Garmin Connect or Apple Health. Its offline puzzle features are independent of the endurance-training products.

Purchases and data control

If optional premium subscriptions are offered, payments will be handled through Apple's App Store. Any final export, deletion or account controls will be explained in the released app and updated policy. GamaCoraggio does not plan to sell puzzle data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.
