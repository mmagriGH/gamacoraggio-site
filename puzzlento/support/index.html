PUZZLENTO · SUPPORT

Help, without the noise.

Puzzlento has nine offline logic games, a gentle daily featured puzzle and an optional lifetime collection unlock.

How do I play a new game?

In Puzzles, tap Learn beside any game for a step-by-step visual lesson. Each game also has a quick How to Play reference.

Do I need internet?

Puzzle play and saved progress work offline. App Store purchases and purchase restoration may require network access.

How does the free daily puzzle work?

One featured puzzle is available every day at no charge, alongside 27 starter puzzles that are always free. An unfinished daily puzzle can be resumed later. There are 243 premium puzzles in the first collection, so daily selections will eventually repeat.

How do I restore the full collection?

Open Puzzlento Settings → Your Collection → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account originally used to buy the one-time lifetime unlock. If the App Store is unavailable, try again when connected.

How do I remove saved progress?

Removing Puzzlento from your iPhone deletes its local app data, subject to any device backup copies you may retain. Apple purchases are associated with your Apple Account and can be restored separately.
