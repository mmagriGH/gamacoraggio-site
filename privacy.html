PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Effective date: October 22, 2026

GamaCoraggio LLC respects your privacy. This notice describes the information handled through our corporate website and outlines how we intend to handle Garmin Connect data if optional integrations are introduced into our applications.

Corporate website

Our corporate website is informational and does not require account creation. Our website hosting and security services may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, and request logs to deliver, protect, and maintain the website. When you email us, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond to your inquiry.

The corporate website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process technical request information in connection with website delivery and security.

Application-specific privacy practices

This corporate notice is not a replacement for each application's privacy policy. Product-specific notices explain the data types handled by each app, purposes, third-party providers, retention, and available user controls.

  • EnduroPace (available): EnduroPace Privacy Policy. EnduroPace includes performance and race-planning features, optional read-only Apple Health context, and an optional paired Apple Watch companion. In EnduroPace 1.6, TelemetryDeck product analytics remains off until the athlete explicitly chooses to share anonymous usage analytics. Review its product policy and in-app permission settings for current practices.
  • SudoWise: Sudoku Coach (available): SudoWise Privacy Policy. Puzzle history and learning progress are designed to remain on the device by default. The App Store privacy disclosure identifies device identifiers and product interaction data used for analytics and not linked to identity; review the product policy for more information.
  • EnduroStrength (in development): EnduroStrength pre-release privacy notice describes the planned local training data, optional Apple Health, proposed product analytics, and Garmin integration. It will be updated with confirmed processes before release.
  • Puzzlento (in development): Puzzlento pre-release privacy notice covers the planned offline-first experience, anticipated product analytics, and future subscriptions. Final practices will be confirmed before release.

Where an app requests access to sensitive information, such as Apple Health or Garmin Connect data, access should be optional and limited to the functions enabled by the user. Data obtained from Apple Health is not intended for advertising or marketing uses. For currently released functionality, consult the applicable app-specific policy, in-app settings, and App Store privacy disclosures.

Analytics in currently released applications

EnduroPace and SudoWise use TelemetryDeck for product-usage analytics, such as feature navigation, engagement and subscription interactions. EnduroPace 1.6 requires an explicit analytics choice before TelemetryDeck can start; SudoWise follows the analytics behavior described in its own product policy. TelemetryDeck may process an anonymized installation identifier and general app/device details. The apps' specific policies explain the actual collection, analytics controls and third-party retention; this corporate notice does not supersede them. Data from Apple Health or future Garmin connections is not intended to be included in product-usage event properties.

Planned Garmin Connect integrations — not yet active

GamaCoraggio is exploring optional Garmin Connect integration for EnduroPace and EnduroStrength. These integrations are subject to Garmin approval and development and are not currently available. The corporate website itself does not connect to Garmin accounts or receive Garmin device-sourced data.

If an integration launches, users will choose whether to connect their Garmin account through Garmin's authorization process. We would request only the permissions needed for the features the user enables. Depending on permissions and available APIs, information may include completed activities and workout details, training metrics, heart rate, sleep and other supported wellness measurements. Compatible planned workouts may also be sent to Garmin at the user's request.

How Garmin data would be used

Authorized Garmin data would be used to provide features such as training summaries, endurance planning, and relevant strength-training context within the connected applications. We do not intend to sell Garmin device-sourced data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. We do not plan to send Garmin-sourced health or activity records to third-party AI or advertising platforms.

Security and service providers

A direct Garmin API integration may require secure cloud infrastructure for account authorization, access tokens, delivery notifications, and limited data processing. The integration hosting provider and final technical architecture have not been selected. Before enabling Garmin connectivity for customers, we will update the relevant product privacy disclosures to identify applicable service providers, the categories of data processed, and relevant safeguards.

Retention, disconnection, and deletion

We intend to retain Garmin-sourced data only as needed to provide connected features, fulfill applicable obligations, and support user-directed data management. Specific storage and retention periods will be documented before launch.

If Garmin connectivity becomes available, users will be able to withdraw access by disconnecting their Garmin account through the available account controls. Disconnecting will stop future authorized synchronization, but it may not automatically delete data previously imported into an app. We will explain how to remove previously synchronized data before the feature launches. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information, subject to legal and technical requirements.

Updates and privacy questions

We may update this notice as our products and integrations evolve. Changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact legal@gamacoraggio.com.
